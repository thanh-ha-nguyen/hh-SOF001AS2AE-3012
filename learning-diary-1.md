# Learning Diary Part 1

## Introduction

I am currently over 40 years old and have more than 17 years of experience in software engineering. Throughout my career, most of my projects have focused on designing and developing database systems for enterprise applications. Because of this background,
many of the terms and concepts introduced at the beginning of this course felt very familiar and easy for me to grasp.

My primary goals for joining the Business Information Technology (BIT) program at Haaga-Helia UAS, and this Data Management and atabases course in particular, are as follows:
-   Efficiency: I want to complete my studies as quickly and effectively as possible.
-   Networking: I aim to build professional relationships that will support my future career here in Finland.

Although I have extensive experience, most of my knowledge is self-taught through books, technical documents, and practical work rather than formal education. By participating in this program, I hope to gain deeper insights from experts in the field. I am looking for diverse perspectives and new approaches to data management that I may not have encountered during my years of self-directed practice.
